LakeWatch Bengaluru

Open-water and vegetation extent of 34 Bengaluru lakes, measured from Sentinel-2 each winter (Jan-Feb), 2018-2025. Lake outlines are coloured by their 2018-2025 open-water trend: shrinking, growing, or no clear trend. Click a lake for its numbers.

How far to trust this

  • Water areas run low. On 12 hand-labelled test lakes, the median open-water area error was 24% against a target of 10% (missed). Dark, deep water is the main cause: the index used here (MNDWI > 0) calls some of it land.
  • Interval coverage 83% (target 80-100%); pixel overlap with hand labels: water IoU 0.78, vegetation 0.74.
  • 8 winters is too few to call a trend for most lakes. No clear trend means the data can't tell, not that the lake is stable.
  • One person drew all hand labels; there is no second-annotator check yet.
  • Extent only: nothing here measures water quality or explains causes.
