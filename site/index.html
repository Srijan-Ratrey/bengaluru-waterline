Bengaluru Waterline

About Bengaluru Waterline

How much open water and how much weed cover each lake had every winter (Jan-Feb), measured from 10 m Sentinel-2 satellite images. Water is where MNDWI > 0; vegetation is NDVI > 0.5 inside the lake outline. Trends also use a second, pre-monsoon reading each year (Mar-mid May). Every number has a range, and the method was checked against hand-drawn maps of lakes it was never tuned on.

Lakes: 34 were drawn at random from all named OpenStreetMap lakes of at least 2 ha (12 per size band), so they stand for Bengaluru's lakes in general. 10 well-known lakes the draw missed were added on request and are marked added on request; they are not part of the random sample.

How far to trust this

  • Water areas run low. On 12 held-out test lakes the median water-area error was 24%, against a 10% target (missed). Dark, deep water is the main cause: the water index calls some of it land.
  • The range around each area held the hand-labelled value 83% of the time (target 80-100%).
  • 8 years is too few to call a trend for most lakes, even with two readings a year (they rise and fall together, so the second adds little). No clear trend means the data can't tell, not that the lake is stable.
  • Possibly shrinking / growing (dashed label): the change is significant for that lake on its own (p < 0.05), but not after allowing for testing 44 lakes at once. Treat these as leads: about 2 of the 44 would get the label by chance.
  • Only the winter readings were checked against hand labels; the pre-monsoon ones use the same method unchecked.
  • One person drew all hand labels; there is no second-annotator check yet.
  • Extent only: nothing here measures water quality or depth, or explains causes.
